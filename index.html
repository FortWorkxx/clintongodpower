COMMON QUESTIONS

GOOD TO KNOW.

Can’t find your answer? Get in touch

How do I request a quote?

Complete the quote form and send the prepared message in WhatsApp. Include your vessel, engine model and what you need so we can discuss the scope and pricing.

Which outboard brands can I enquire about?

Send us the make, model and horsepower of your outboard. We’ll discuss available service or equipment options for your setup. The site does not list confirmed stock or brand partnerships.

Where are you based?

CGI Marine is based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. Please contact us to confirm where and when to meet, or to ask about support in your area.

How do I arrange a visit or payment?

Call or message first to confirm availability, the scope of work and payment terms. This website does not take bookings or payments directly.
